About ER Professional Development

I am a practising criminal lawyer with extensive experience of advocacy, leadership, managing teams, supporting staff wellbeing and making difficult decisions in demanding environments.

My interest in psychology began with my studies in 1998. My work now brings together professional experience, psychotherapy, solution-focused practice and hypnotherapy-informed techniques where I am currently undertaking further professional training in this area..

The approach is straightforward: less jargon, no unrealistic promises about removing pressure, and practical ways to help people access what they already know and do their best thinking when it matters.
